**Reading:**
- **Averaging models of the same family** (seeds, folds, all-data) reduces noise, and is worth about +0.003 for a
  *single* model. Inside a blend that already averages 3–4 families it shrinks to about 0–0.001.
- **New families with different errors** are what moved the blend (+0.002 to +0.006).
- **EXP-059** (blend + all-data Raptor, pending) is the direct LB test of the all-data idea.

## 2. Why all-data ConvNeXt + a second all-data Raptor seed could help, and the honest size of it
- **More data per model:** each fold model sees 80% of the studies; an all-data model sees 100%. All-data Raptor
  alone = 0.930, vs 0.928 for one fold and equal to the 5-fold ConvNeXt ensemble.
- **Different errors:** different data subsets and seeds make different mistakes (rank corr ~0.95). Averaging
  cancels part of them: +0.003 for one family in isolation (table above).
- **Inside our blend the same averaging is already happening across families,** so the expected LB gain is
  **+0.000–0.002**, near the 0.001 display resolution.

**Decision rule:** if EXP-059 (adds all-data Raptor at 0.15) beats 0.936, more all-data models are worth about $4.
If it stays at 0.936, skip them.
