## 4. CV–LB
* ConvNeXt vs EfficientNet: fold 0 ΔOOF +0.007, Δgold +0.020, ΔLB **+0.016** (0.923 vs 0.907); 5-fold ΔOOF +0.010,

* Fold 0 vs fold 1 alone: OOF +0.016 but LB +0.001 — per-fold OOF reflects each fold's studies, not model quality.
* Ensembling gain is smaller than for EfficientNet: 5-fold vs single fold +0.002–0.003 LB (EffNet: +0.007–0.009),
  gold +0.005 vs +0.016 — ConvNeXt folds agree more with each other.
