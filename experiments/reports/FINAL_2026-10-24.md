## Three phases

1. **Own pipeline (09-19 → 10-10), LB 0.914 → 0.941.** DICOM → fixed-size uint8 slice stacks cached on Kaggle (14 tar shards,
   checksum-verified), 2.5D windows of 3 adjacent slices, per-finding attention-MIL; trained on rented RTX 3090s (vast.ai),
   inference on Kaggle T4s. Gains came from backbone + resolution (EfficientNet-B0 @224 → ConvNeXt-S @288, +0.012), denser
   slices (+0.004), and reproducing the public Raptor (CoAtNet) and reader (ConvNeXt-T transformer-MIL) recipes for family
   diversity (+0.006); 96-slice native-384 Raptor v2 all-data seeds reached 0.941 alone.
2. **Public-model era (10-09 → 10-18), 0.948 → 0.958.** With external data allowed when it needs no institutional sign-off
   (host ruling, discussion 741819) the strongest public models became usable for pick 1: nartaa's 0.949 CoAtNet (OAI-trained) as the anchor, heliosli's compact ConvNeXt-S (R7 → R6 + flip TTA,
   fastMRI auxiliary), goodpjw's 2.5D reader at small per-finding weights. Our contribution on top was per-finding routing
   between the two families (+0.001), verbatim-source external stages with sha-checked weights, and a rule-safe second pick.
   Our own models no longer added anything to this stack (EXP-094..096).
3. **ITER-14 (10-18 → 10-19): nartaa's S6 recipe retrained** ([ITER-14_plan.md](ITER-14_plan.md)). Built heliosli's px cache
   for all 4,321 training studies on Kaggle (5 shards, 0 errors, 71 GB JPEG q95, no DICOM download), trained ConvNeXt-S with
   nartaa's exact argv (seed 43) on one RTX 4090 in 3.6 h (loss curve identical to theirs, gold-58 0.929 vs their 0.932,
   0.934 averaged). Added to the stack it held 0.958 / 0.942.

## What worked

- **Report-derived soft labels + the 58 gold studies as a fixed holdout** (fold −1, never trained on): one split for every run
  (`configs/folds_5.csv`), so every experiment is comparable.
- **Gates before submissions**: fold 0 first vs the current best fold 0; checkpoint-marker verification in `submit.sh`
  (EXP-017 had silently scored stale weights before it existed).
- **Rank fusion per finding** with verbatim external stages (nartaa, heliosli, goodpjw) behind sha256 checks — no re-hosting of
  third-party weights, every public number reproduced before it was blended.
- **Infrastructure**: preprocessed caches built on Kaggle and pulled as checksummed shards (never the 570 GB of DICOMs);
  `fetch_kernel_output.py` (48 threads: 2.8 → 73 MB/s from a far box); experiment registry matched to the LB by the `EXP-NNN`
  prefix of every submission message.

## What did not work

- **Pseudo-label students**: the first (EXP-010, 50/50 report + teacher OOF) was worse than its teacher; later students were
  roughly neutral (ConvNeXt student fold 0 EXP-078 0.929 vs the dense fold 0 at 0.926; Raptor v3 student in EXP-082 0.941 = the
  family without it) and never moved the final stack (EXP-094 0.953 vs EXP-093 0.954).
- **New backbone families on our cache**: DINOv2-S, RadImageNet R50 (EXP-120..122), gold-58 0.84–0.86 vs 0.914 for the base;
  every blend weight lowered gold.
- **Our models inside the public stack** (EXP-094..096): neutral to −0.001.
- **Tuning per-finding weights on gold-58**: 58 studies with 9–35 positives per finding cannot rank recipes (noise ±0.02);
  goodpjw's gold-tuned weights also lost on the board (0.957 / 0.955 vs 0.958).
- **A second compact model next to R6**: heliosli's S6 timed out (EXP-126, 4 folds @352 + side model ≈ R6's cost); S6fall
  fitted but changed nothing (EXP-127/128).

## Why no medal — reading

- The decisive gain (+0.02) came from public models every top team also used: ~940 teams printed 0.956 from the same weights
  (leoprovorov's analysis, SUBMIT_QUEUE_2026-10-17). A medal needed signal the public stack did not have; our own families
  topped out at 0.941 public / 0.925 private, below the single 0.949 public CoAtNet, so they could not supply it.
- The public split's SD (~0.003) was larger than every recipe difference we tested after 10-15; from then on the work was
  bounded by noise, not by ideas.
- With more time, the remaining levers were the ones that add independent signal: more seeds / different label sets for the
  S6 recipe on the cache we built, flip TTA for S6, and a stronger own backbone trained on the 96-slice cache.

  (1× 4090, ~4.9 h incl. setup); earlier iterations carry planned costs ($1–6 each) in their reports. No instance is running
