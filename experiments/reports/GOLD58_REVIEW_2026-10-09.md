# Gold-58 AUC in discussions and public code (2026-10-09)

Scope: all 147 competition discussion topics (full comment text) and the 43 most-voted public notebooks that mention
"gold 58" (of 200 matches), plus our own registry. Tools: `$CLAUDE_JOB_DIR/tmp/all_topics.py`, `gold_grep.py`,
`gold_kernels.py`, `gold_nb_grep.py`.

## 1. What the 58 studies are
- 58 / 4,407 studies have all 12 expert labels; the rest have only reports (734106).
- **Not a random sample:** ACL prevalence 41% vs ~20% in the corpus; every gold study has at least one positive
  (734106; karnakbaevarthur EDA). Few positives per finding: MCL 9, Lateral OA 11, Baker's 12 (747540).
- Bootstrap 95% CIs on single findings are wide (Lateral OA 0.66–0.96); macro CI about ±0.04 (wguesdon notebook).
- JEV published 16 more labelled studies (gold-74). Several public notebooks also use a 45-study "panel" subset.

## 3. How public code uses gold
- **Selection:** nartaa 0.949 and Raptor pick epochs on gold ("useful diagnostics, not independent CV"; epoch 14
  raised gold while losing on LB — 746792). heliosli blend-gold: "Raptor ... selected on the 58 gold, so no clean
  labelled set exists to fit [blend weights]".
- **Blend weights tuned on gold:** the frontier/E10/E13 family (`score = macro_auc(gold_y, blend) - 0.01*alpha`),
  "gold-gated" readers (kozykappa), JEV labels (per-finding member weights from gold AUC). The bee's-knees notebook
  reads the six public 0.950 notebooks as using hand-set or gold-tuned per-label weights. JEV: per-finding blending
  on gold overfits.
- **Training with gold upweighted:** loss weight ×2 (DINOsaur v5), ×3 (enhanced ensemble, resnet-v4, read-the-report),
  ×8 (wguesdon DINOv2). Then gold is no longer a held-out ruler.

## 4. Our own data (registry, 20 scored experiments)
- All: Pearson gold vs LB **0.988**, LB − gold = **+0.0225 ± 0.0046** (range 0.77–0.915 drives the correlation).
- Competitive range (gold ≥ 0.895, n = 14): Pearson **0.74**, Spearman 0.64; report-label OOF vs LB 0.62.
  Fit LB ≈ 0.65 × gold + 0.337.
- Prediction for tomorrow: EXP-064 (gold 0.9137) → ~0.932, EXP-065 (0.9142) → ~0.932. **Both were selected by gold**
  (best-3 epochs), so their gold is optimistic; expect at or below this.

## 5. Takeaways
1. Gold-58 is the best local LB proxy we have (better than report-label OOF), but at our level a 0.005 gold gap is
   within noise; it ranks directions, not close calls.
2. Any gold use for selection or weights (our best-3-by-gold, per-finding weights) spends the ruler: report the
   result as biased and confirm on LB.
3. Pair gold with pooled 5-fold OOF on report labels (n = 4,349) for decisions; keep per-finding weights uniform
   (JEV and ours: +0.0001).
