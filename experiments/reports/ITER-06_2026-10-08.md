## Reading
- **Raptor recipe:** best single fold-0 CV so far (+0.0025, within noise). Converges slowly (OneCycle with
  2.4 warm-up epochs, backbone LR 3e-5): 0.821 at epoch 4 vs 0.855 for ConvNeXt. Scoring every window position
  gives +0.006 over 24 windows.
- **Reader recipe:** converges fastest (0.872 at epoch 4) and has the best gold of any single fold 0 (0.915 at
  epochs 7–9). It finished lower (0.907) because the EMA weights kept drifting after the peak with no top-k
  selection. Its CV on our targets is lower partly because it is trained on a different label set.
- **Diversity:** the three pipelines are clearly different, and the three-way blend adds **+0.008 CV / +0.012
  gold** over ConvNeXt alone. That is about 3× the noise floor and the largest fold-0 gain since the dense cache.

## Engineering notes
- **Raptor at batch 8 OOMed in Lightning** although the probe peaked at 20.6 GiB (a 3.38 GiB block did not fit).
  Fixed with `PYTORCH_CUDA_ALLOC_CONF=expandable_segments:True`. The reader needs gradient checkpointing at batch 8.
- **`train_pl.predict` now uses the OOM-safe forward.** CoAtNet × 78 windows × 4 studies does not fit in one pass.
  `predict_only=true` rebuilds predictions from an exported fold; EXP-041's were rebuilt this way.

### Raptor
- **Not overfitting.** Train loss falls every epoch (0.919 → 0.691) and validation keeps rising until the LR
  anneals out. It is limited by learning rate and time, not data.
- **Where the time goes:** the slow start is OneCycle's 2.4-epoch warm-up from peak/25 with a backbone LR of 3e-5.
  Epochs 12–15 (~1 h per fold) add only +0.0014.
- **Compute-bound:** GPU at 100%, 12.4 min of training + ~2 min validation per epoch. Gradient checkpointing is
  required at batch 8 (20.6 GiB with it). A bigger batch or dropping checkpointing does not fit a 3090.
- **Options for folds 1–4:**

| Option | Change | GPU time, 4 folds | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 (safe) | identical to fold 0 (LB 0.928) | ~17 h, ~$4.2 | none |
| R2 | `training.num_epochs=13`, same OneCycle shape | ~14 h, ~$3.4 | small, expected ≤ 0.002/fold |

  Faster schedules (backbone LR 5e-5 to 1e-4, 1-epoch warm-up) are untested on Raptor. Our CoAtNet with a cosine
  schedule and 1 warm-up epoch plateaued at epoch 9, but its data and augmentation also differ. Only worth a fold-0
  test if GPU budget allows.

### Reader
- **It overfits after epoch 9** (train loss keeps falling while val and gold drop), and the exported last-epoch EMA
  lost −0.001 val and **−0.009 gold** vs the peak.
- **`configs/exp/reader_v2.yaml`:**
  - top-3 averaging of the EMA weights (tested: `test_e2e_reader_v2_ema_topk`);
  - 11 epochs;
  - our default label blend instead of the 4-table mean. The reader had the lowest CV and LB, and labels are its
    main difference.
- **Cost:** ~1.9 h per fold. Run fold 0 first (vs EXP-042: CV 0.8690 / LB 0.922), then folds 1–4 of the better
  version (~7.6 h).
