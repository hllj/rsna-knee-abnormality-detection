What the numbers say

Gold-58 is our best single predictor of the LB

With all blends re-scored on gold through the submission pipeline, gold explains most of the LB spread (r 0.93, typical error ±0.0019). The earlier estimate of r 0.74 mixed gold numbers from different scoring paths.

The error is systematic by model family, not random

The 96-slice Raptor v2 models land +0.003 to +0.004 above the line (EXP-068, EXP-074), as does Raptor repro fold 0 (EXP-045). The readers (EXP-046, EXP-056) and CoAtNet-dense fold 0 (EXP-040) land 0.005–0.008 below it. Gold underrates the Raptor family and overrates the readers. Compare candidates within a family, or add the family offset.

Tuning weights on gold overfits it

EXP-076 has the highest gold of any submission (0.9259) because its weights were chosen on those 58 studies, yet it scored 0.940, below the plain two-seed average EXP-074 (0.941). Its residual is negative while EXP-074's is +0.003.

OOF is a reliable ruler inside one fold

Fold-0 OOF tracks the LB with r 0.81 and a slope near 1. Fold 1 is an easier fold: EXP-008, EXP-021 and EXP-029 read about 0.010–0.012 higher OOF than their fold-0 twins for the same or a lower LB.

Gold cannot judge the OAI-anchored blends

Every nartaa-anchored submission (pink) lands 0.005–0.008 above the gold line: nartaa alone reads 0.9229 on gold but 0.949 on the LB. Inside that group gold ranks them backwards. EXP-077 (nartaa + our seeds) has the best LB, 0.949, with mid-range gold; the routed blends EXP-085/086 have the highest gold, 0.928, and score 0.948. For these blends only the LB decides. They are plotted but left out of every fit.

The two local scores see different things

Gold and OOF correlate only r 0.41. The pseudo-label ConvNeXt (EXP-078) shows why. Its OOF rose +0.008 on report labels and its gold barely moved (+0.0007), yet the LB rose +0.003. The LB sided with OOF here, and with gold for the family offsets above.

How to use each score from here

Recipe or architecture change

Train fold 0 and compare fold-0 OOF on report labels (gate −0.003), then check gold has not dropped by more than ~0.01. Never compare OOF across folds.

All-data models

Gold is the only local signal and is optimistic when it also picked the epochs. Compare against the same family's gold, not across families, and confirm on the LB.

Blend weights and members

Do not tune weights on gold. Use prior weights and let the LB decide; a predicted gain below ~0.002 is inside the noise and not worth a submission on its own.

Reading a gold gap

One gold point ≈ 0.63 LB points. A 0.004 gold gain predicts only ~+0.0025 LB, about one tick, before the family offset.
